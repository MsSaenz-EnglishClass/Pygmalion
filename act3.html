Act III, page 9 of 10

Quick check

Four questions about what happened. Pick an answer to see if you’re right.

1. Where does Higgins bring Eliza for her first test?

Mrs. Higgins’s afternoon visiting day, which she told Higgins not to come to!

2. What two topics is Eliza supposed to stick to?

Higgins thought those two topics would be “safe.” His mother disagreed.

3. What goes wrong at the party?

Higgins fixed HOW she speaks, but not WHAT she says, just like he warned in Scene 1.

4. What worries Mrs. Higgins most?

Eliza will have a lady’s manners, but no lady’s money and no way to earn a living.

Act III in one sentence

At Mrs. Higgins’s party, Eliza sounds perfect but says shocking things, and Mrs. Higgins asks the big question: what will happen to Eliza when the experiment is over?
