Act IV, page 9 of 10

Quick check

Four questions about what happened. Pick an answer to see if you’re right.

1. Did Eliza pass the big test?

Pickering tells Higgins he has “won your bet” and that “Eliza did the trick.” Notice who gets the credit.

2. Why is Eliza so angry?

Higgins says “Thank God it’s over!” and the whole thing “has been a bore.” Nobody thanks her.

3. What does Eliza ask Higgins before he goes to bed?

She gives back the rented jewels and the ring he bought her, and Higgins is furious.

4. How does Eliza feel after Higgins storms out?

For the first time all night, she smiles. Then she goes looking for the ring.

Act IV in one sentence

Eliza wins Higgins’s bet for him, but when he and Pickering take all the credit, she fights back and asks what will become of her now.

Coming up in Act V

The next morning, Higgins bursts into his mother’s house in a panic: Eliza has run away in the night! Where did she go?
