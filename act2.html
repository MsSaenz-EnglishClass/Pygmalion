Act II, page 9 of 10

Quick check

Four questions about what happened. Pick an answer to see if you’re right.

1. Why does the flower girl come to Higgins’s house?

She wants to “talk more genteel,” so a real flower shop will hire her.

2. What does Pickering bet?

The loser pays for the whole experiment. Pickering also offers to pay for her lessons.

3. How long will Liza live and study at Higgins’s house?

That’s the deal: six months of lessons, then a trip to Buckingham Palace to see if she passes.

4. What does Liza’s father, Alfred Doolittle, want from Higgins?

Higgins even offers ten, but Doolittle takes only five, so the money won’t make him “prudent.”

Act II in one sentence

Liza comes to Higgins to buy speaking lessons, Pickering bets that Higgins can’t pass her off as a duchess, and her father lets her stay for five pounds.

Coming up in Act III

Months later, Higgins takes Eliza to his mother’s house for her first big test in front of real high-society guests. Will anyone guess she used to sell flowers?
