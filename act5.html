Act V, page 9 of 10

Quick check

Four questions about what happened. Pick an answer to see if you’re right.

1. Where did Eliza go when she ran away?

She was upstairs the whole time Higgins was calling the police.

2. Why is Doolittle so unhappy about being rich?

Thanks to Higgins’s joke letter, a millionaire left him a fortune. Now everyone wants his money, and he has to get married.

3. Eliza says the difference between a lady and a flower girl is…

Pickering always treated her like a lady. Higgins always treated her like a flower girl.

4. How does the play end?

Higgins chuckles, sure she’ll do what he says anyway. Shaw doesn’t tell us if she comes back.

Act V in one sentence

Eliza stands up to Higgins, proves she can live without him, and leaves for her father’s wedding, while Higgins stays sure she’ll come back.

What happened next?

So many people assumed Eliza would end up with Higgins that Shaw wrote an extra essay to set them straight: she marries Freddy, and with Pickering’s help they open a flower shop.

All character lines are quoted exactly from the public-domain text of Pygmalion (Project Gutenberg eBook #3825). Some scenes skip lines to keep things moving. The “Plain English” versions and narration are written for this zine.
