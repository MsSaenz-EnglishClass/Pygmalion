Quick check

Four questions about what happened. Pick an answer to see if you’re right.

1. Why is everyone stuck under the church porch?

A surprise summer storm hit right when the theaters let out, so every cab was taken.

2. Why does the flower girl panic about the man with the notebook?

Trouble with the police could ruin her reputation, and her business.

3. How does the note taker know where people are from?

It’s phonetics, “the science of speech.” (The boots were the Bystander’s idea!)

4. What does Higgins brag he could do in three months?

He claims that changing how she talks could change how the whole world sees her.

Act I in one sentence

On a rainy night, a rude speech expert named Henry Higgins meets a poor flower girl and brags that he could make her pass as a duchess, just by changing the way she talks.
